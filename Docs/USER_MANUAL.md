# Ultimate Penalty Cup 3D — User Manual

## Welcome

Welcome to **Ultimate Penalty Cup 3D**, the ultimate penalty shootout experience! You play as **Rubén González (#10)**, a rising football star competing in 5 rounds of increasingly intense penalty kicks against the legendary goalkeeper **Malágon**.

---

## Getting Started

1. Launch `UltimatePenaltyCup3D.exe`
2. The **Main Menu** appears with the game logo
3. Click **JUGAR** (Play) to start

---

## Main Menu Options

| Button | Function |
|---|---|
| **JUGAR** | Opens Round Select — pick which round to play |
| **TIENDA** | Shop where you buy balls and stadiums with coins |
| **PERSONALIZACIÓN** | Equip your purchased items |
| **LOGROS** | View your achievement progress |
| **OPCIONES** | Adjust music and sound effect volumes |
| **SALIR** | Quit the game |

---

## How to Play a Round

1. Select an unlocked round from the Round Select screen
2. You have **5 penalty attempts** per round
3. For each attempt:
   - **Move** your player with WASD/Arrow Keys to adjust your angle
   - **Aim** at the goal by moving the mouse — a crosshair shows where you're targeting
   - **Hold Left Mouse Button** to charge the power bar
   - **Release** to kick the ball
4. The goalkeeper will try to save your shot based on the round difficulty
5. Score enough goals to pass the round and unlock the next one

---

## Scoring

- **Normal Goal**: 100 points + 100 coins
- **Corner/Precision Goal** (aim near the edges): 200 points + 150 coins
- **High Power Goal** (charge to 90%+): 150 points + 125 coins
- **Streak Bonus**: Score consecutive goals for +50 pts / +25 coins per streak level

## Achievements

There are 7 achievements to unlock:

1. **Primer Gol** — Score your first goal
2. **Francotirador** — Score 10 corner/precision goals
3. **Racha Perfecta** — 5 consecutive goals in one round
4. **Campeón** — Win the Gran Final (Round 5)
5. **Millonario** — Accumulate 10,000 coins
6. **Coleccionista** — Unlock all 5 balls
7. **Leyenda** — Unlock all other achievements

---

## Tips & Strategies

- **Aim for corners**: The goalkeeper has less reach at the edges — corner goals earn double points
- **Watch the power bar**: Green is safe, yellow is strong, red is maximum — but don't overcharge!
- **Build streaks**: Consecutive goals give escalating bonus rewards
- **Upgrade your ball**: Different balls have different trail effects (cosmetic only)
- **Pick your stadium**: Each stadium has unique lighting and atmosphere
- **In later rounds**, Malágon reacts faster — use corner shots and vary your timing

---

## Save Data

Your progress is saved automatically:
- Coins, points, and high score
- Unlocked rounds, balls, and stadiums
- Equipped items
- Achievement progress
- Audio settings

Data is stored locally via PlayerPrefs (JSON serialization).
